FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

A few useful points before you contact us.

What kind of businesses can contact Sarovaram?

Business owners, MSMEs, entrepreneurs, property owners, developers and investors can contact us to discuss their requirement. The available support depends on the specific case.

Does Sarovaram provide loans directly?

No. Sarovaram Fincorp is a loan facilitation service, not a lender. Any approval, rate, fee, amount and repayment terms are subject to the relevant lending partner's assessment and final decision.

Can I enquire about a business loan or loan against property?

Yes. You can share a brief overview of your requirement. Any possible financing route depends on eligibility, documentation, property details where applicable and the lender's policies.

Does Sarovaram guarantee funding or a transaction?

No. An enquiry or advisory process does not guarantee funding, loan approval, a property transaction, an investment, a partnership or any particular outcome.

What should I prepare before contacting you?

Start with your business or project overview, purpose, approximate requirement and timeline. Share sensitive financial or identity documents only through an appropriate, secure channel when requested.

Is there a minimum requirement for Capital Advisors?

Please confirm the applicable minimum ticket size or project threshold directly with Sarovaram Capital Advisors before relying on a fixed amount.
